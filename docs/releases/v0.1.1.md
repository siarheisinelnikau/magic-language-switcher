**Customizable shortcuts without a larger settings window.**

### New

- Click any shortcut button in settings and press the combination you want.
- Release the keys to save. **Esc** cancels; **Backspace** restores that action's default.
- Choose Ctrl/Alt/Shift combinations, function keys, and supported toggle keys.
- Duplicate action bindings and shortcuts already used by other apps are rejected.
- A failed registration or settings write restores the previous binding.
- Custom shortcuts survive restarts; changing layouts keeps shortcut preferences intact.
- Undo hints show your chosen shortcut.

The settings window remains **464 × 526 logical pixels**. Existing settings upgrade automatically and keep the original F8, Shift+F8, and Ctrl+F8 defaults until you change them.

### Download

Run **`MagicLanguageSwitcher-v0.1.1-windows-x64.exe`** — a single portable EXE for Windows 10 version 1607+ and Windows 11 x64. No installer, .NET, or VC++ redistributable is required. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
